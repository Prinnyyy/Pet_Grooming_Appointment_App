# Beckon Launch Film

## Style Prompt
An understated product launch on a pure white stage. Real iOS Simulator footage is the product, framed by a thin phone silhouette. Chinese editorial titles guide attention; the native English interface remains untouched. Intentional camera moves and steady reading pauses, no visual clutter.

## Colors
- Stage: #FFFFFF. Main text: #333333. Secondary: #6F767E.
- Customer: #93CEC2, strong #518B7F. Groomer: #FF9A8B, strong #F58575.
- Preserve the native app background and source color profile. Final output Rec.709.

## Typography
PingFang SC for Chinese; system sans-serif for Latin. Brand 88px, headings 64px, supporting 40px, role labels 32px. Letter spacing 0. Explicit line breaks, at most two title lines. No web-font download.

## Layout and Motion
1920x1080, 30fps, 60 seconds. Horizontal safety 96px, vertical 72px. Native-aspect phone about 900px high. Text left, phone right; magnify genuine interface detail where required. One focus at a time. Text reveal 0.3-0.5s; camera 0.4-0.6s. Titles steady at least 2s. Booking confirmation and final message hold 3s.

## What NOT to Do
- No fabricated interface, success state, rating, logo, or one-tap claim.
- No decorative orbs, particles, gradients, textures, bounce or spinning phones.
- No narration, background music or effects in v1.
- No second Dynamic Island over captured iOS footage.
- Never hide the real offer confirmation step or imply instant service completion.
